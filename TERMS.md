# Terms of use

UniPaper Bridge helps users locate bibliographic metadata, find reported open-access copies, and construct user-controlled institutional access links. It does not grant access rights.

Users must:

- Use only accounts and institutional entitlements they are authorized to use.
- Follow the relevant library, publisher, copyright, and licence terms.
- Avoid credential sharing, access-control circumvention, automated bulk downloading, and redistribution of licensed files.
- Verify bibliographic and open-access metadata before relying on it.

The software is provided without warranty. Institution adapters are informational and may change; the institution's current official guidance controls.
